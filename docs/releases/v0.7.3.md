# DSH-Bot 0.7.3

当前为 `dsh-botgroup@0.7.3` 发布候选。以下更新相对于[公开版 0.7.2](https://github.com/MJ-CJM/DSH-Bot/releases/tag/v0.7.2)（2026-11-21 发布）。本版安装包已冻结，校验和与同包复验结果见下文。

## 群聊里完成转问、审核和阅读

- **直接问群外 Bot，或请成员代问。** 在普通群说「问 Jarvis：迁移还有哪些待办？」，由 Jarvis 回答；说「让通用助手问 Jarvis 迁移还有哪些待办？」，则由通用助手取得答案后回群回复。也可以回复该成员的消息请它转问。`@` 菜单只列出本群可用成员和 `@everyone`。
- **看清实际转出的内容。** 群内可展开实际送出的问题、群外 Bot 的中转答复和成员最终回群内容。双方主会话的收发标记只展开对应一次投递获准查看的原文。说「这个」而话题不清时，先补充愿意分享的主题，也可填入引用摘句、编辑后发送。
- **普通问答免审，私有资料按接收者确认。** 延续 0.7.2 的普通问答规则；本版将它用于群外转问。私有内容先交给受托成员、再分享到全群时，分别核对准确正文和读者。只批准前一步或拒绝群回传，都不会把受保护正文放进群时间线或详情。
- **群执行限制到自身获准工具。** 群成员、群外受邀 Bot 和恢复的群任务不能通过文件读取、Shell 或通用会话工具读取其他 Bot 的私聊日志。记忆访问仍按各 Bot 的身份与权限核对，任务失效后保留限制。
- **同轮一起审核，阅读位置保持稳定。** 集中预览同一轮、同一批读者的多条待审回复，取消勾选不想发送的条目后确认本次发送。审核期间新到的回复留给下次，不自动加入已预览批次。新回复到达时保留已展开消息和阅读位置。
- **记录记忆更顺手。** 输入「只记下来：要记录的内容」，或引用一条群消息再说「只记下来」。内容不明确时保留草稿并提示补充。也可从输入框 `+` 菜单选择「只记录记忆」，通过可取消标签确认本次操作；各成员分开保存私有记忆，群里只报告写入状态。
- **群管理与设置更直接。** 管理页和右侧成员栏均可添加成员并同步名单，显示当前人数与实际上限，沿用最多 6 位 Bot 的限制。群可置顶、取消置顶或删除；删除会移除群目录项并归档原生会话历史，成员 Bot、私聊和私有记忆保留。有未结束执行或关联自动协作任务时，会说明暂不能删除的原因。「Bots → 我的 Bots → 自动协作」直接显示当前状态，点开即可修改。
- **长回复按需展开。** 提问和每条回复可单独展开、收起。多人回复时，长回复默认折成一行；短回复和单独点名的答复默认展开。同轮收到至少两条 Bot 回复后，可一起展开或收起。

0.7.2 已提供的普通 Bot 互问免审、上一轮唯一已交付结果复用及 13 项巡检修复继续保留，不计作本版新增。

## English

This is the `dsh-botgroup@0.7.3` release candidate, compared with [published 0.7.2](https://github.com/MJ-CJM/DSH-Bot/releases/tag/v0.7.2), released on November 21, 2026. The archive is frozen; its checksum and package-specific verification are recorded below.

- **Ask outside the group, directly or through a member.** Ask Jarvis directly for a reply under Jarvis's name, or ask a group member to consult Jarvis and report back. The `@` menu lists available group members and `@everyone`.
- **Inspect the actual exchange.** Expand the question sent out, the outside Bot's answer and the member's final group reply. Main-conversation markers show only the authorized text for that delivery. An unclear “this” prompts for a topic or an editable quote excerpt before sending.
- **Keep ordinary questions simple and private sharing explicit.** The approval-free ordinary questions introduced in 0.7.2 also apply to group relays. Private content passed to a member and then to the group requires separate confirmation of the exact text and readers. Withholding the group reply keeps protected text out of the group timeline and details.
- **Limit group execution to authorized Bot tools.** Members, invited Bots and resumed group tasks cannot use file, shell or general session tools to read another Bot’s private conversation logs. Memory access remains scoped to each Bot, and the restriction remains after a task expires.
- **Review a round together.** Preview replies for the same round and readers, deselect individual replies, then approve this send. Later arrivals wait for the next review. New replies preserve expanded messages and the reading position.
- **Record memory from the composer.** Use a “record only” instruction with text or a quoted message, or select **Record memory only** from `+`. A removable tag confirms the mode. Unclear references preserve the draft. Members save separate private memories; the group receives status only.
- **Manage groups and collaboration in place.** Add members from management or the member sidebar, with synchronized rosters and visible capacity. The existing six-Bot limit remains. Pin, unpin or delete a group; deletion archives native conversation history while preserving member Bots, private chats and private memories. Active work or managed collaboration blocks deletion with a reason. **Bots → My Bots → Automatic collaboration** shows the current state and opens its setting.
- **Expand long replies when needed.** Fold individual questions and replies, or all replies in a round. Long replies in multi-Bot exchanges start collapsed; short replies and individually addressed answers start expanded.

Approval-free peer conversations, eligible previous-answer reuse and the 13 inspection fixes already shipped in 0.7.2 are retained, rather than counted as new features.
